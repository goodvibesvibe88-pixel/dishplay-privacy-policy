Features

What's inside

Coming soon · iPhone

Your recipes, your shopping list and your kitchen, in one app that speaks your language.

  • What can I make?

    Type the ingredients you have and get a recipe to cook with them.

  • Find a recipe

    Look up a dish by name and keep the one you like in your collection.

  • Cook mode

    Follow the recipe one step at a time in large type. The screen stays on while you cook.

  • Shopping list

    Pick a few recipes and DishPlay gathers their ingredients into one list you can share.

  • Search, rate and sort

    Type "spinach" and every dish that uses it shows up. Stars mark your favorites.

  • In English and Spanish

    The app opens in your phone's language. It also speaks French, Portuguese, Italian and German.
