Privacy policy

DishPlay is made by Krissia Portillo Duran. This policy describes the information the app keeps and the information it sends.

Effective 19 November 2026

  1. 01 Information on your iPhone

    DishPlay can be used without an account. Recipes, photos you add, your shopping list, and your language choice remain on the iPhone. Checks marked while cooking are kept only for that visit.

    The camera and photo library are used only when you add a picture to a recipe.

  2. 02 Account and sync

    Sign in with Apple provides the name and email address on your Apple account, so the app can recognize your account. If Google sign-in is offered, Google provides the name and email address on that Google account.

    Signing out hides recipes on that iPhone. Recipes already stored with the account remain there.

    DishPlay Plus can sync recipes, notes, and recipe photos through Google Firebase. They are stored with your account so they can appear on your other devices. Sync remains off until you turn it on.

  3. 03 Recipe information sent to other services

    • What you can make. The ingredient list you type is sent to Google, which returns a recipe.
    • Translation. The title, ingredients, and instructions are sent to Google. Notes are not included.
    • Generated photos. The recipe title is sent to OVHcloud. If OVHcloud is unavailable, the same title is sent to Pollinations. The photo is returned to your iPhone.
    • Find a recipe. The dish name you type is sent to TheMealDB, which returns public recipes. A result is saved only if you choose to keep it.

  4. 04 Purchases

    DishPlay Plus is sold by Apple. Apple processes the payment. DishPlay does not receive your card number. Apple tells the app whether a subscription is active.

  5. 05 Information we do not collect

    DishPlay does not sell personal information. It does not show advertising, and it does not use a tracking or analytics service. It does not request microphone access.

  6. 06 Children

    DishPlay is not directed to children under 13.

  7. 07 Deletion

    To delete your account in the app, open Settings, tap Use on another phone, then Delete account. This removes the account and the recipes and photos stored with it. You can also ask by emailing henryelrocker@gmail.com from the address on that account. Recipes that exist only on an iPhone are removed when the app is deleted.

  8. 08 This website

    These pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Opening them sends the usual connection data, including an IP address, to Google. The DishPlay app does not load those fonts.

  9. 09 Changes and contact

    If this policy changes, the effective date above will change with it. Questions can be sent to henryelrocker@gmail.com.
